# Design context

The creator asks for a distinctive ancient-architecture visualizer that teaches
ancient Greek vocabulary through pictures and diagrams. Learners are expected
to be fluent or native English speakers; prior Greek is not required. The creator
explicitly rejects generic showcase filler and values substantial learner action.

## Product decision for this workshop

Read selected clauses of the Piraeus naval-storehouse programme and make a plan
that accounts for their measurements. Learn why an inclusive preposition changes
the floor area, why one exceptional segment changes a pillar, and why satisfying
selected measurements leaves multiple plans possible. Source, derived arithmetic
and modern drawing choices are always visible as separate kinds of evidence.

## Design direction

An architectural measuring sheet: warm limestone ground, deep brown ink, brick
red correction marks and olive green verified dimensions. Large geometric plates,
ruled dimension lines and generous margins. Avoid catalogue cards, decorative
classical imagery, ornate invented capitals or photorealistic reconstructions.
Greek is integral to the working drawing. Text remains readable at 375 pixels;
exact values also appear as ordinary text beside the diagrams. Native controls,
keyboard operation, no motion required, no remote font or tracking dependency.
